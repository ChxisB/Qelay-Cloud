# Known issues in v1.0.0

Everything we know is open, unproven or limited in this release, in one place. [limits.md](limits.md) holds the measurements
behind most rows, [unsupported.md](unsupported.md) the Qelay features that are not here, and
[the security review](security/review-v1.0.0.md) the findings (F-1 to F-8).

**What is not on this list.** Delivery is at least once by design: a lease that expires while a job runs hands the job to
another worker, so a handler must be idempotent ([concepts](concepts.md#a-jobs-life)). That is a guarantee, not an issue.
No issue below is known to lose a waiting, delayed or active job. One replaces a finished job's record (section 1, row 2).

## 1. Open, and waiting for the owner's decision

| 1 | **A page of payloads can still decode to a lot of memory** (security finding F-2, medium, narrowed). Enqueue, data edit and completion now refuse a payload or result of more than 20,000 values (`maxPayloadNodes`) or nested past 64 (`maxPayloadDepth`), so one decodes to at most about 1.4 MB (measured 70 bytes a value at the worst). The list route still decodes a whole page before cutting each payload to 1,024 bytes. | A page of 500 worst-case jobs takes about 700 MB, where one job of a million empty arrays took 70 MB each before. Jobs enqueued before the limit are not rechecked. The 20,000 is a choice (the review suggested 250,000, which is about 17 MB a payload). | Keep `QCLOUD_API_MAX_PAGE_SIZE` small ([limits section 13](limits.md#13-workflows-in-the-control-api-and-the-panel-task-62)). Lazy decode in the list route is not done. |

| 12 | **The public ingest (D12) has had a self-review only, and has never faced the internet.** Its limits count per process (`QCLOUD_INGEST_RATE_GLOBAL` 6,000 and `QCLOUD_INGEST_RATE_PER_SOURCE` 600 a minute). (c) A signed request carries no timestamp the ingest checks, so a captured one can be posted again. | 14 | **A queue route can add its job twice** (event gateway, D11). The job's id is `qcloud-route-<delivery id>`, so a repeated attempt finds the job and adds no second, but only while it still exists: one that finished and was removed or replaced before the repeat is added again (the same id rule as row 2). | A lapsed claim that comes back after the job ran to its end runs the target's handler twice for one event. The route's `dedupe` rule drops repeated *events* before they are recorded; it does not make a repeated *attempt* of one delivery safe, so it does not close this. |

## 3. Limits you will meet

- **Flows** hold at most 1,000 jobs and 100 levels. A child that fails with no failure policy holds its parent until the
  parent is cancelled. Cancelling or removing a parent leaves its children as ordinary jobs.
- **Webhooks** are at least once with no ordering. A backlog that outgrows the sender's rate is dropped after 24 hours.
  Finished deliveries are kept 2 days (`QCLOUD_WEBHOOK_KEEP_MS`). Delivery history from before migration 13 is not carried
  over: the old table `qcloud_webhook_deliveries` is empty of live rows and is dropped by a later release (a migration
  never drops what the previous release still reads). A webhook is paused after 20 failed deliveries in a row
  (`QCLOUD_WEBHOOK_DISABLE_AFTER`, 0 is off); nothing is paged, the signal is the `webhook_failing` and `webhook_paused`
  warnings and the `webhooks.paused` gauge. A store with a `QCLOUD_WEBHOOK_KEY` signs every new webhook (a secret is
  generated and returned once when none is given); webhooks made before stay unsigned until rotated. The panel's create
  form shows no generated secret and has no rotate button: use `POST /webhooks/{id}/rotate-secret`.
- **Workflows**: the Control API holds no definitions, so an operator action is a claim plus a publish, and a lost publish
  waits up to five minutes for the tick. The tick skips `waiting` runs; a single-run recover reaches them. Compensation
  handlers are at least once across a crash. A sub-workflow polls and holds a worker slot. A run is read whole per request.
- **Tracing** covers `Queue.add` and `addBulk` only, with no `tracestate` or baggage.
- **Connections**: `Bun.SQL` opens all of `max` at once, so the budget is spent at start.
- **Namespaces** are a convenience for environments, not a tenant boundary ([threat model](security/threat-model.md#namespaces)).
- **Public exposure** is the control-plane default, with no web application firewall (F-4). Viewers see payloads (F-7).
- **Licences**: seven shipped packages carry no licence file in their tarball, so [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) lists them by the licence they declare.
