**Decisions** (details in the task's Result and the gate document)

- Every method is async, with the `*Async` twins of Qelay's TCP client. Qelay's `host`, `port`, `connection`, `embedded`, `dataPath`,
  `autoBatch` and `prefixKey` are refused by name; `defaultJobOptions` stays.
- A `Queue` sees only its own queue (ids are per namespace). A custom id held by a live job of another queue is refused.
- `getJobs` is bounded (default 100, at most 1,000, `-1` and `asc` refused); `getJobCounts` shows the paused view; there is no unbounded read.
- A ring failure, whole or partial, is logged, counted and swallowed; the job stays unrung for the tick. `resume` rings nothing.
- Stall config takes `maxStalls` only; DLQ config takes `maxEntries` and `maxAge` only. DLQ retry, groups, schedulers, dedup-key calls,
  `getMetrics` and `durable` stay closed, each with its reason in `docs/compat.md`.
- The plan's method names are aliases of Qelay's, so either spelling works.

**Bugs the tests found (fixed)**

- Ring counters were counted twice in `ring.ts`.
- `Queue.addJobLog` threw `UnsupportedOptionError` for a missing job where `job.log` threw `JobNotFoundError`; both go through `jobOps.addLog` now.
- Shared transport bells leaked between tests: the fixture renews the transport per test.
- The fixture passed `databaseUrl` and `namespace` together with a borrowed `store`, which the SDK rejects; it omits them now.
- Two of my own test expectations were wrong, not the code: `attemptsMade` is 0 while a job is active (the store counts failed attempts),
  and the default `maxStalls` is 3.
