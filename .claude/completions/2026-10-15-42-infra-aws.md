**Measured**

- Connection budget (AWS counts four control processes at pool 2, plus 6 reserved): 34 direct connections at 10 workers, 114 at 50, 414 at 200

  module agree for every combination in the check.

**Decisions**

- The tick on AWS is a one-shot Fargate task (`qcloud-tick run`) started every minute by EventBridge Scheduler: `serve` verifies Google OIDC
  tokens. `maximum_retry_attempts = 0` and an event age of 60 s, so a missed minute is not replayed; the `tick-stalled` alarm covers it.
- `control_processes` defaults to 4 on AWS; the shared budget module takes it as an input and refuses a fleet over `max_connections`.
- RDS Proxy is off by default and unmeasured, as task 05 and spike 05 said; on, workers also receive a direct `QCLOUD_DATABASE_LISTEN_URL`.
- Egress defaults to a NAT gateway with SQS and Secrets Manager endpoints; `egress = "none"` needs five endpoints and the plan refuses fewer.
- Queue names, tags and settings equal `ensureQueue` and `queueNames`, plain and hashed; Terraform adds KMS, a redrive allow policy and queue
  policies. Adding a queue is an infrastructure change.
- Every role is separate and every statement scoped. The only `*` resources are `ecr:GetAuthorizationToken`, `cloudwatch:GetMetricData` and,
  with the AWS-managed key behind an RDS Proxy, the proxy role's `kms:Decrypt` on `key/*` in the account.
- The plan assertions are the policy scan, because no policy scanner is installed.

**Found and fixed**

- A hashed-name plan failed a precondition because the test's override dropped the queue the autoscaled service scales on; the driver now keeps
  it. The IAM assertions passed 300 lines and were split into modules. A redirect to an unset scratch variable failed once.
- Terraform could not plan an ARN built from a resource that did not exist yet (a `jsonencode`d policy with one unknown leaf is wholly unknown),
  so ARNs are built from the account id and names, and the queue key's presence is a variable known at plan time (`queue_key_enabled`).
- Moving the budget module out of `infra/gcp` could have broken the GCP check; `check:terraform` was re-run in full afterwards (GCP 110).
